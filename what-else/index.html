💡 What Else?

One everyday object. How many uses can you invent?

How it works

Three rounds, three everyday objects. You get 60 seconds per object — shout out (or type) as many different uses as you can. Repeats don't count, and wild ideas score just as much as sensible ones.

Each round earns 0–3 stars: 8+ ideas is 3 stars. A perfect game is 9 stars. Play solo, or pass the phone around and brainstorm as a team.
