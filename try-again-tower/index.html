Try Again
Tower

Build a tower to reach the top. Your first try will probably fall down — and that is exactly how you learn! Every fall reveals a clue about the tower’s secret rules. Change your plan, try again, and build it smarter.

How it works

  • 1. Build — tap blocks to stack them up to the goal height.
  • 2. Fall & learn — when the tower falls, you get a clue in your notebook. Falling is never a punishment — it’s information!
  • 3. Build smarter — use what you learned to change your plan and try again.
  • Scoring — fewer tries and more smart changes = more points. Each clue you actually use in your winning tower earns a +10 Smart Change bonus.

Blocks: 🧱 Square (weight 2, wide & heavy) · 🪵 Long plank (weight 1, wide, spans gaps) · 🟡 Triangle (weight 1, light) · 🪨 Heavy stone (weight 3, very heavy) · 🪧 Round log (weight 1, light). Brace a block to hold it steady against wind and wobbles — but braces are limited!

Keys: 1–5 place a block · B brace · U undo · Enter try again / continue.
