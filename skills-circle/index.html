⭕ Skills Circle

12 rounds, one per human skill. One phone, 2–8 players, pass and play.

How it works

Your team plays 12 rounds — one for each skill in the Human Skills Framework: Critical Thinking, Creative Thinking, Systems Thinking, Communication, Social Awareness, Collaboration, Self-Awareness, Self-Management, Self-Efficacy, Curiosity, Resilience, Perseverance.

Each round scores 0–3 stars. At the end you get a Skills Wheel showing your team's strengths and growth areas — saved on this device so next time you can see how you've grown.

An informal, formative measure for families and classrooms — not a validated assessment.
