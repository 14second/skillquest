🗺️ Pass-and-Play Quest

Two players. One phone. Half a map each.

How it works

You and a partner are treasure hunters with a torn map — each of you holds half the clues. Pass the phone back and forth to read your secret clues, then combine them out loud to find the treasure on the island grid.

5 islands to explore. Each island: find the treasure (2 stars) and solve the guardian's riddle (1 star). That's 15 stars for a perfect quest.

Rule of the game: you may say your clue out loud — but never show the phone to your partner until it's their turn!
