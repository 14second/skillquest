Program a robot, debug your commands!

Build a program from blocks, press ▶ RUN, and watch your robot follow it step by step. If it bumps a wall or misses the goal, change your program and try again — that's debugging!

⭐ Pick up every star with the ✋ Pick Up block, then finish standing on the 🏁 goal. 🔋 Batteries are a bonus. 🔁 Loops repeat blocks — fewer blocks can earn more stars!

Tap a block in your program to remove it. Keys: ↑ forward, ← → turn, P pick up, Enter run.

1️⃣ Tap command blocks to add them to your program.
2️⃣ Forward moves one square. Turn Left / Right spins the robot in place.
3️⃣ Pick Up grabs a star or battery on the robot's square.
4️⃣ Loop ×2 / ×3 / ×4 / ×5 repeats everything up to End Loop.
5️⃣ Press ▶ RUN to watch it play, or STEP to run one block at a time.
6️⃣ Finish your program standing on the 🏁 with all ⭐ collected. Shorter programs earn more stars — 3 stars means you matched the cleverest solution!
