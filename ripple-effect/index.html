🌊 Ripple Effect

One small change ripples through a whole system.
Can you predict the ripple?

How to play

  1. Look at the pond, garden or school system and read the event.
  2. Tap the parts you think will change. Tap again to switch between ▲ up and ▼ down.
  3. Press PLAY ▶ and watch the ripple travel step by step.
  4. Compare your prediction with what really happens — and find out why.
