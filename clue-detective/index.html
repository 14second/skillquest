How to play

  1. Read the mystery and the clues. Every case has exactly one answer.
  2. Tap a grid square to mark it: ✗ means “not this one”, ✓ means “yes, this one”.
  3. Use the clues to rule suspects and facts in or out. A ✓ fills in the rest for you.
  4. When you know who did it, tap Accuse under their name.
  5. Stuck? Use a Hint — it reveals one true fact. Hints and wrong accusations cost points.
  6. Solve 5 cases to finish the set. Keyboard: 1–5 accuse, H hint, M mute.

Solo game · Grades ~1–12 · Original stories, no licensed characters.
