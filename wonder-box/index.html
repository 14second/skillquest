What’s hiding in the box?

A mystery is waiting — a rattling box, a sideways plant, a footprint that wasn’t there yesterday. You get a few question tokens. Ask good questions — specific, testable ones that narrow the possibilities — and each answer hands you a clue.

Waste tokens on vague questions and you’ll run out before you know the answer. When you think you know it, tap your guess. A wrong guess costs a token too!

Good detectives ask one thing at a time: What does it look like? What happened just before? What can I test? After each mystery you’ll see why the good questions worked.
